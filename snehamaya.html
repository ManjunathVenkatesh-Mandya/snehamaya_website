About Us

Snehamaya Foundation is a community-focused organization working with queer people and allies. We aim to create safe, supportive spaces and improve awareness, inclusion, and access to community resources.

What We Do

Community Support

Creating welcoming spaces where queer individuals can connect, share experiences, and find support.

Awareness

Promoting understanding and respectful conversations about gender identity, sexual orientation, and inclusion.

Empowerment

Supporting individuals through educational resources, community programs, and opportunities for personal growth.
